Meet Samantha

Fifteen years in wellness, and a mum who has been through it herself.

I am a qualified Living Nutrition Practitioner with over fifteen years in the wellness industry. I support people of all ages, and I have a particular interest in children's nutrition and nutrition before, during and after pregnancy.

That interest is personal. I became a mother in May 2020 and experienced first-hand how hard it is for new mums to fit in time for their own health. When my son was unsettled and uncomfortable, and I was told it was "just colic" that he would grow out of, I used my training to find the root cause and improve his symptoms.

Whatever you or your child are facing, I recommend booking a 15-minute consultation first, and we can discuss any tests or further support needed.

Testing partners

Testing is arranged through these specialist laboratories.

GutMapDx

Specialists in personalised nutrition for gut health. Their tests are only available through practitioners, not direct to the public. Tests are done at home and analysed by their partner laboratories under strict quality control. Results are checked against their database of more than 150,000 samples and over 150 published studies, giving clear reports that are easy to act on and a personal nutrition plan.

Cambridge Nutritional Sciences (CNSLab)

Makers of the FoodPrint® food sensitivity tests, with over 35 years in food sensitivity testing. Tests are developed and made at their UK laboratory in Cambridgeshire, to ISO standards.
